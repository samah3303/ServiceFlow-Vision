CAMERA CALIBRATION

Teach each angle the floor plan

For each camera, select at least four points on the floor that you can also locate on the restaurant map. The system uses those pairs to translate a person’s position in the image into a position on the shared plan.

  1. Choose points spread across the visible floor, not clustered together.
  2. Click a point in the camera image, then click the same place on the map.
  3. Repeat for every camera. Six to eight points usually produce a stronger calibration.

A camera that cannot see the floor around a person’s feet may need a different calibration method later.
